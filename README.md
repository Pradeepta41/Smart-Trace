SMART TRACE
The Digital Red-Thread of Fate
Overview

SMART TRACE is an end-to-end pharmaceutical serialization and traceability solution designed to prevent
counterfeiting and improve supply-chain visibility. The system manages high-volume unique ID generation,
packaging hierarchies, product verification, and field-level tamper detection.

Key Features
GS1-Compliant Serialization: Generates unique identifiers for individual units, cartons, and pallets through a 3-tier serialization workflow.
Hierarchy Management: Maps and visualizes parent-child relationships between units, cartons, and pallets for end-to-end traceability.
Validation & Verification: Verifies whether products are correctly associated with their respective packaging hierarchy.
Anti-Counterfeit Detection: Uses counters, check digits, and verification mechanisms to identify duplicate, invalid, or suspicious product IDs.
Scan-and-Verify: Provides a web-based interface for scanning and authenticating pharmaceutical products.
Location-Based Validation: Detects potential supply-chain anomalies by comparing product locations against expected movement-distance thresholds.

Objective

The goal of SMART TRACE is to create a traceable and verifiable pharmaceutical supply chain, enabling manufacturers and stakeholders to authenticate products while identifying potential counterfeiting and movement anomalies.
